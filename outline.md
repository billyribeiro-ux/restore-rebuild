# The thirty-day map

Working reference while writing. Each chapter pairs one verse with one mechanism — never two mechanisms in a day.

## Rules for the writing
- One mechanism per chapter. No stacking.
- The second-person opening must be specific and different every day — no two chapters may open on the same image.
- Affirmations are welded to that day's verse *and* mechanism. Never generic.
- No promise of returns. No implication that faith guarantees profit.
- Where evidence is contested, say so in the chapter.
