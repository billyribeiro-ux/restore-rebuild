## If you are thinking about ending your life

Losing a great deal of money is one of the recognised triggers for suicidal thinking. Researchers have a name for the events most likely to precede it — sudden financial loss sits on that list alongside bereavement and public disgrace. If you are a trader who has just blown an account, you are, statistically, in a raised-risk window right now. That is not said to frighten you. It is said so that if the thought has crossed your mind, you know it is a known feature of what you are going through and not evidence that something is uniquely wrong with you.

If you are having thoughts of harming yourself, this book is not enough, and it is not the right tool. Please contact a crisis line today.

- **United States and Canada** — call or text **988**
- **United Kingdom and Ireland** — call **116 123** (Samaritans)
- **Australia** — call **13 11 14** (Lifeline)
- **Brazil** — call **188** (CVV)
- **Anywhere else** — findahelpline.com lists a free service in your country

And then tell one human being who knows your name. Not a forum. A person.

Money is recoverable. There is a version of this in which you are, five years from now, telling somebody about the worst week of your life while sitting comfortably in a life you rebuilt. That version requires you to be here for it.

## What this book is, and is not

This book is a psychological and spiritual programme for recovering from a trading loss. It is written by a performance psychologist, drawing on published research in cognitive and behavioural science, and on scripture.

It is **not** financial advice, investment advice, or a trading strategy. Nothing in it should be read as a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold anything, or to return to trading at all. Appendix A describes a graded re-entry protocol; it is a psychological risk-management framework, not a suggestion about what to trade or how much capital to commit. Those decisions are yours, and if the sums are serious they belong in a conversation with a licensed financial professional who knows your circumstances.

It is also **not** a substitute for clinical care. If you are experiencing persistent depression, an anxiety disorder, addiction — including a gambling disorder, which is common among traders and frequently unrecognised — or thoughts of self-harm, please work with a licensed therapist or physician. This book sits alongside treatment perfectly well. It does not replace it.

Finally: nothing here promises you will make money. No book can promise that, and any that does is selling you something. What is on offer in these pages is a rebuilt relationship with risk, with yourself, and with God. Whether the market pays you afterward is not in my gift, or in yours.

## About the scripture

The verses in this book are taken from the **World English Bible (WEB)**, a modern-English translation in the public domain. Where a familiar phrase reads better in the King James Version, that is noted at the verse.

I have tried throughout to let each passage say what it actually says in its own context, rather than pressing it into service as a slogan. Scripture is not a performance-enhancement supplement, and a book that treats it as one does a disservice to both the text and the reader. What you will find instead is something I have come to believe after years of this work: that the oldest descriptions of the human heart under pressure and the newest measurements of the human brain under pressure keep arriving at the same place by different roads.

If you are not a person of faith, you are welcome here, and the psychology stands on its own. Read the verses as what they have been for three thousand years — the most durable record we have of people surviving ruin. You will not be asked to sign anything.
